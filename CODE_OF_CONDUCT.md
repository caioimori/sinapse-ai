# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the SINAPSE AI community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and respect.
- Welcome different perspectives and experiences.
- Give and receive constructive feedback professionally.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect private information and disclose conflicts of interest.
- Focus discussion on the work and the health of the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, insults, threats, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Retaliation against a reporter, reviewer, or moderator.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in repository issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews,
community channels, events, and whenever someone officially represents the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For abusive or unacceptable behavior, contact repository owner
[@caioimori](https://github.com/caioimori) through a private channel available on
GitHub. Do not publish sensitive incident details in a public issue. GitHub's
native **Report content** feature is also available for content hosted on GitHub.

For software vulnerabilities, use the separate
[private security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect the privacy and safety of the
people involved, avoid conflicts of interest, and apply proportionate action.
Possible consequences include correction, warning, temporary restriction, or
permanent removal from project spaces.
