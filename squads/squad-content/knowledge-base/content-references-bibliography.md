## 1. Pessoas-Chave na Historia do Conteudo

| Pessoa | Contribuicao | Obra Principal |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| **David Ogilvy** (1911-1999) | Pai da publicidade moderna. Pionerou copy baseado em pesquisa | "Ogilvy on Advertising" (1983) |
| **Eugene Schwartz** (1927-1995) | Maior copywriter de resposta direta. Teoria dos niveis de consciencia | "Breakthrough Advertising" (1966) |
| **Claude Hopkins** (1866-1932) | Pioneiro de publicidade cientifica. Copy testavel e mensuravel | "Scientific Advertising" (1923) |
| **Seth Godin** (1960-) | Permission marketing, Purple Cow, tribo. Prolific blogger | "Permission Marketing" (1999), "Purple Cow" (2003) |
| **Joe Pulizzi** | Fundador do CMI. Cunhou "content marketing" como disciplina | "Epic Content Marketing" (2013), "Content Inc." (2015/2021) |
| **Ann Handley** | Chief Content Officer do MarketingProfs | "Everybody Writes" (2014) |
| **Robert Rose** | Chief Strategy Advisor do CMI. Content-as-business-asset | "Killing Marketing" (2017, com Pulizzi) |
| **Kristina Halvorson** | Mae da content strategy. Fundou Brain Traffic e Confab | "Content Strategy for the Web" (2009/2012) |
| **Donald Miller** | Criador do StoryBrand framework | "Building a StoryBrand" (2017) |
| **Marcus Sheridan** | They Ask You Answer methodology | "They Ask, You Answer" (2017/2019) |
| **Gary Vaynerchuk** | Content repurposing model. Social media strategy | "Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook" (2013) |
| **Gary Halbert** (1938-2007) | Lendario copywriter de direct mail | "The Boron Letters" (postumo) |
| **Dan Kennedy** | Direct response marketing. Info-marketing pioneer | "The Ultimate Sales Letter" (1990) |
| **David Perell** | "Writing on the Internet" como skill critica | Write of Passage (curso online) |
| **Tiago Forte** | Building a Second Brain. Knowledge management para creators | "Building a Second Brain" (2022) |
| **Tim Ferriss** | Pioneiro em podcast long-form como conteudo de autoridade | The Tim Ferriss Show (podcast) |
| **Robert Cialdini** | Psicologia da persuasao aplicada a marketing e copy | "Influence" (1984), "Pre-Suasion" (2016) |
| **Joseph Campbell** (1904-1987) | Monomito/Jornada do Heroi — base de todo storytelling | "The Hero with a Thousand Faces" (1949) |
| **Ryan Holiday** | Marketing perene, estoicismo aplicado a criacao | "Perennial Seller" (2017) |
| **Joseph Sugarman** | Slippery slide. Cada frase vende a proxima | "The Adweek Copywriting Handbook" (2006) |
| **Chip & Dan Heath** | Mensagens memoraveis. SUCCESs framework | "Made to Stick" (2007) |
| **William Zinsser** | Clareza, simplicidade, humanidade na escrita nao-ficcional | "On Writing Well" (1976) |
| **Jonah Berger** | Ciencia do viral. STEPPS framework | "Contagious" (2013) |
| **Nancy Duarte** | The Gap — tensao entre presente e futuro | "Resonate" (2010) |
