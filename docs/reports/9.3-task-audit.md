A follow-up could normalize these headings or expand the audit to accept more variants.

2. **`## Metadata` (12%)** -- Primarily affects squads that embed metadata in frontmatter rather than a dedicated body section. The 6 squads scoring 100% (brand, cloning, content, courses, finance, product) have both frontmatter and body metadata.

3. **`Entrada` / `Saida` (6%)** -- All 70 affected files are the 26 `claude-code-mastery` tasks (which had no frontmatter at all and received a minimal stub) plus 44 other tasks that use alternative field names.
