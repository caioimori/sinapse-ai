## Strategic Context

`squad-artdir` v1.0 was born to solve one problem: landing pages that convert. Reference case: `nyo.ia.br`. Delivered 10 agents, 7 Pillars, 7 tasks, 5 KBs, 2 workflows — all tuned for marketing surfaces (LP / site / hero / CTA / CRO).

**Gap identified (Caio, 2026-04-12):** SINAPSE is being used increasingly to build **full SaaS platforms, dashboards, logged-in products**, not just landing pages. The current squad-artdir framework degrades outside the LP context because:

1. **No product-surface IQ** — Canvas & co optimize for first-visit conversion, not for daily-use retention in logged-in apps
2. **No scalable design system** — tokens are optimized per-LP, not versioned across product + marketing surfaces
3. **No platform aesthetic canon** — no reference library for "what a premium SaaS looks like" (Linear, Vercel, Stripe, Framer, Arc, Raycast)
4. **No premium packaging** — no framework to justify charging 3x more than a competitor through visual/experiential signals

## Business Outcome

Enable SINAPSE users (starting with Caio's portfolio: Colegio Modulo, Astro Brand Studio, @caioimori, SINAPSE itself) to ship SaaS products and dashboards that **look and feel premium from day one**, so pricing power is earned through design — not discounted through commodity aesthetics.

**North-Star Metric:** At least one real SaaS/dashboard (Chrome Brain UI, SINAPSE platform, or client product) shipped with a documented art direction brief generated by v2.0 agents.

## Scope (IN)

- Benchmark of 6 canonical premium SaaS references (Linear, Vercel, Stripe, Framer, Arc, Raycast)
- Extension of the 7 Pillars framework to 10 Pillars (adding 3 new pillars for platform surfaces)

  - `product-surface-director` (Axiom)
  - `design-system-architect` (Atlas)
  - `platform-aesthetic-director` (Vertex)
  - `premium-packaging-strategist` (Aura)
- 6 new tasks covering product surface, design system DDL, premium packaging
- 2 new knowledge bases: `saas-art-direction-canon.md`, `premium-packaging-principles.md`

- Updated `squad.yaml` (v2.0.0) and `README.md`
- Updated `artdir-orqx` (Canvas) routing to dispatch platform briefings to the 4 new agents

## Scope (OUT)

- Building the actual SaaS products (this epic only delivers the squad, not client deliverables)
